how it works

You remember it. We go looking.

No saved searches to babysit, no endless scrolling at 1 a.m. Just one patient little agent with a very good memory.

📸
1

Describe your treasure

Upload an old photo (or a picture of one like it), tell us the year, the quirks, the colour of the tag. Set what you’d pay.

🔎
2

We hunt daily for 90 days

Every morning the agent sweeps eBay, Poshmark, Mercari, Vinted, Etsy, FB Marketplace and Yad2 — and keeps a log you can read.

💌
3

You make the call

Promising matches arrive with a confidence score. Tap “This is the one!” and you’re reunited. You buy direct from the seller.
