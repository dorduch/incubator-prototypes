By our Senior Felt Correspondent

It was 11:47pm, the pot was 2,400 chips, and Yossi Ben-Ami had a plan. The plan, sources confirm, was "vibes."

Holding the worst starting hand in poker, Yossi pushed his entire stack into the middle, leaned back, and attempted what three separate witnesses describe as "a casual whistle." No sound came out.

"He looked me dead in the eye and said 'I've never been more sure of anything.'" — Dana

Dana, holding pocket queens, called instantly. Yossi then turned over his cards "like he was defusing a bomb," per Itai, who has since asked to be named as a hero in this story. He is not.

At press time, Yossi was claiming the bluff was "a long-term investment in table image." Our analysts estimate the return on that investment at negative 2,400 chips. Continued on p.2 → "Is Yossi OK?"
